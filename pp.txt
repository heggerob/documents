PRIVACY POLICY
SangBoken – Digital sangbok
Effective date: 2026-05-31
Operator: Robin Hegge
Contact: heggerob@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we process when you use SangBoken –
Digital sangbok ("the Service"), how it is used, and your rights under applicable law
including the EU/EEA General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Norwegian personal
data legislation (personopplysningsloven).

1. WHO WE ARE
The Service is operated by Robin Hegge (personal data controller).
Contact: heggerob@gmail.com

2. DATA WE PROCESS

A) Authentication and identity (when you sign in)
- Email address and basic profile from Apple or Google
- Firebase Authentication identifiers and session data
- Authentication provider (Apple or Google)

B) User preferences and private content
- Display and application settings stored on your device and optionally synchronized
  via the Service
- Private notes, personal song sequences, and favorites you explicitly choose to save
- Region or country selection

C) Interaction data
- Song likes and reactions you submit
- Support and feedback messages you send through the Service

D) Technical and diagnostic data
- Anonymized usage and error data needed for reliability and security
- Server access logs (IP address, timestamp, endpoint) retained for a maximum of
  30 days for security purposes

E) Local-only device data
- Preferences, bookmarks, and caches stored only on your device are not accessible
  to us unless you initiate a sync

3. PURPOSES AND LEGAL BASES
We process your data to:
- Provide, maintain, and improve the Service (contract performance / legitimate interest)
- Authenticate users and protect account security (legitimate interest / legal obligation)
- Synchronize your private state across your devices (contract performance)
- Investigate abuse and enforce these policies (legitimate interest)
- Comply with legal obligations (legal obligation)

4. DATA SHARING
We do not sell personal data.
Information may be shared with:
- Google Firebase / Google Cloud as a data processor (authentication, database,
  hosting) under standard contractual clauses
- No other third parties receive your personal data without your explicit consent,
  except as required by law

5. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS
Firebase services may process data in data centers outside the EEA. Such transfers are
protected by standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

6. RETENTION
- Authentication and account data: retained while your account is active; deleted within
  30 days of account deletion upon request
- Private content (notes, sequences, favorites): deleted immediately upon your request
  or account deletion
- Server logs: maximum 30 days
- Support messages: retained until the matter is resolved, then deleted within 90 days

7. YOUR RIGHTS (GDPR)
Depending on your location, you may have the right to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
- Request restriction of processing
- Data portability
- Object to processing based on legitimate interests
- Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority
  (in Norway: Datatilsynet, www.datatilsynet.no)

To exercise your rights, contact: heggerob@gmail.com
We will respond within 30 days.

8. SECURITY
We use Firebase's security infrastructure, HTTPS encryption in transit, Firebase
authentication token verification, and server-side access controls. No system can
guarantee absolute security. In the event of a data breach affecting your rights, we
will notify you as required by applicable law.

9. CHILDREN
The Service is not directed to children below the minimum digital consent age
(15 years in Norway). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe
a child has provided data without consent, contact heggerob@gmail.com.

10. CHANGES
This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date above will
be updated when changes are made. Material changes will be communicated through the app.
