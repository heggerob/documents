COPYRIGHT POLICY
Effective date: February 16, 2026

This policy describes how copyright concerns are handled in the Service.

1. Rights Requirement
Only submit or publish material when you have the legal right to do so.

2. Restricted or Removed Material
Material may be hidden, restricted, or removed when copyright status is
unclear or rights are not properly established.

3. Takedown Requests
Copyright owners or authorized agents may request removal by contacting:
heggerob@gmail.com

Please include:
- Your name and contact details
- Identification of the copyrighted work
- Identification of the material to remove (for example ID, title, link, or path)
- A good-faith statement that use is not authorized
- A statement that the request is accurate and authorized

4. Repeat Infringement
Repeated or intentional infringement may lead to enforcement actions,
including restrictions or removal of access.

5. Counter Notice
If you believe material was removed in error, contact the same address with
sufficient details to review your claim.
